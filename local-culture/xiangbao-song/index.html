坪林相褒歌 Pinglin Xiangbao Song

流傳於北臺灣茶山之間的傳統對唱民謠，記錄先民採茶勞動與日常情感的口傳文化瑰寶。 A traditional antiphonal folk song passed down through the tea hills of northern Taiwan, preserving the oral heritage of tea-picking labor and everyday life.

什麼是相褒歌？ What is Xiangbao Song?

相褒歌是早期臺灣北部山區與茶園常見的民間即興對唱山歌，「相褒」意指彼此讚美、調侃或應答，常在採茶、挑擔或農忙時節傳唱。 (Placeholder) Xiangbao Song is a traditional improvisational antiphonal folk song once common in the tea hills of northern Taiwan. The term "Xiangbao" refers to mutual praise, playful banter, and call-and-response singing during farm work and tea harvesting.

發展背景與歷史 Background & History

隨著清代漢人入墾坪林及文山包種茶產業的興盛，來自閩南與客家的茶農在山林開墾與採茶過程中，將原鄉歌謠結合在地生活經驗，孕育出獨具地方色彩的坪林相褒歌。 (Placeholder) Following Han settlement in Pinglin during the Qing dynasty and the rise of Wenshan Baozhong tea, tea farmers blended ancestral folk melodies with local mountain life, shaping a distinct regional tradition of Xiangbao singing.

歌曲特色與形式 Features & Form

相褒歌多以七言四句為基本結構，講求押韻與聲調平仄，曲調悠揚自由且無須樂器伴奏，演唱者可依當下情境即興編詞、一問一答。 (Placeholder) Typically structured in four-line stanzas of seven characters each, Xiangbao songs emphasize rhyme and tonal cadence. Sung a cappella with free, lingering melodies, singers improvise lyrics in call-and-response exchanges suited to the moment.

歌詞內容與社會功能 Content & Social Function

歌詞題材涵蓋男女情意、茶農辛勞、山林風物與人生哲理；在勞動現場不僅能提振精神、紓解疲憊，也扮演著傳遞地方知識與凝聚社群情感的角色。 (Placeholder) Lyrics span courtship, the hardships of tea cultivation, mountain scenery, and life wisdom. Beyond lifting spirits and easing fatigue during long hours of labor, the songs served to transmit local knowledge and strengthen community bonds.

現代的保存與活化 Modern Preservation

面對農業機械化與世代斷層，坪林社區發展協會與在地耆老透過田野採集、歌謠傳習班及文化導覽活動，致力讓相褒歌在當代重新被聽見與傳承。 (Placeholder) Amid agricultural mechanization and generational shifts, the Pinglin Community Development Association and local elders are working through field recordings, singing workshops, and cultural tours to keep Xiangbao Song alive for new generations.

傳承老師介紹 About the Instructor

林月娥 女士 Ms. Lin Yue-e

生於坪林石𥕢里的林月娥女士，自幼在茶園與稻田間聽著長輩吟唱相褒歌長大。她與先生在坪林老街經營商號並熱心社區事務，因與菊英阿姑對唱結緣，進而投入詞曲創作。林女士歌聲溫婉細緻、節奏穩健，2023 年曾跨界結合嘻哈音樂推廣坪林茶節，2025 年起更在當地開班授課，致力傳承茶山歌謠文化。

Raised in Pinglin's Shicao Village amid tea gardens and rice paddies, Ms. Lin Yue-e grew up listening to elders sing Xiangbao songs. Together with her husband, she runs a local shop on Pinglin Old Street and actively serves the community. Inspired by duet singing with Auntie Juying, she began composing original verses in her gentle, steady vocal style. After a 2023 hip-hop fusion collaboration for the Pinglin Tea Festival, she has been teaching local Xiangbao classes since 2025 to pass on this mountain folk tradition.
